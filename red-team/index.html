Adversarial Review

Red-Team Review: Both Models

I built these models to learn, but a model is only useful if it survives being attacked. I ran an adversarial review on my own work, pretending I was a skeptical Investment Committee member trying to break the thesis before approving the deal. Where a challenge landed, I changed the numbers. Where it didn't, the defense is on the record.

Why this document exists

Investment committees don't take a model on faith. They pay someone to attack it. I did exactly that to my own work. Some attacks landed and I changed the numbers: the Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) rating fell from BUY to HOLD, and the Leveraged Buyout (LBO) headline story got rewritten. Some attacks didn't land, and the defense is on the record. This page is the paper trail of changing my mind for the right reasons.

If you only read two things:

  1. Recordati, challenge B1: The acquisition strategy (buying smaller drug companies) everyone credits for pharma buyouts adds roughly zero to the return rate. The whole investment case is a bet that the exit price holds.
  2. Technogym, challenge A1: My own model was hiding the software business inside a blended margin. Separating it out showed the software is worth ~€3.30 a share, five times what I first claimed. The model beat my own critique.

Structural weaknesses common to both models (recorded, not fixable in-scope)

  1. No mid-year discounting convention (DCF): year-end discounting understates value ~3–4%; direction is conservative, documented.
  2. Provider-triangulated beta, not regression-computed (licensed price series unavailable in build environment).
  3. Grid approximations on both sensitivity sheets, documented in-sheet with bias direction.
  4. No annotated source screenshots: table-level citations substitute; primary PDFs linked in the bibliography.
  5. Aggregator dependence for Recordati 2021–24 historicals (standardised vs company-adjusted EBITDA reconciled by note, not by rebuild from annual reports).
  6. H1-26 results (Technogym, 30 July) and the CONSOB offer document (Recordati, ~Q3-26) both land within weeks: both models carry explicit rebase triggers.
