Investment Summary · Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation · 18 July 2026

Technogym S.p.A. (Milan Stock Exchange: TGYM)

Does a premium equipment maker deserve software company valuation multiples?  ·  HOLD · €15.72
The 30-Second Read

Technogym makes luxury gym equipment but wants to be valued like a software company. The stock fell 30% recently when US growth stalled, so I built a Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model to see if the drop created a buying opportunity. It mostly did not. The equipment business alone is worth roughly today's stock price. The interesting part is the software: valued on its own profit margins instead of blended away, it is genuinely worth about €3.30 a share, but only in the scenario where management finally proves it can grow it. Until they show real software numbers, this is a Hold.

Recommendation

HOLD. Base fair value is €15.72 per share (calculated using the Gordon Growth terminal value method) or ~€18.3 (using an exit multiple method). This compares to the current stock price of €15.19, representing a +3.5% upside. This is below my >20% hurdle for a BUY rating. Conviction is MODERATE (6.5/10). I would buy with a margin of safety if the stock drops below €14.50. A BUY rating requires the upcoming H1-2026 earnings report (due 30 July) to confirm that the Americas growth slowdown has stabilized, plus a credible first disclosure of digital revenue metrics. Bear case target is €8.82 / Bull case target is €24.05. An illustrative probability-weighted fair value (assuming a 40% chance of Bear, 40% Base, 20% Bull) is €14.63, which confirms the HOLD rating.

Business & Moat

Premium Business-to-Business (B2B) fitness equipment franchise (81% commercial revenue) with luxury-brand positioning (they have been the exclusive supplier for 10 consecutive Olympic Games). They use direct field-sales distribution (68% of revenue, meaning they own their own customer relationships). They have an installed base of roughly 100,000 facilities generating recurring service and replacement demand, plus an early-stage connected-wellness software layer. The economic moat relies on brand strength, distribution density, and the cost for gyms to switch providers. The unresolved question, which is the entire stock valuation debate, is whether the digital ecosystem ever makes real money. The company discloses no digital performance metrics (KPIs), so my explicit low-confidence estimate is that digital revenue is currently ~€45m (4.4% of total revenue).

The company has €156m in net cash and no bank debt. Suppliers fund the working capital needs (Days Payable Outstanding is ~221 days, meaning they take a long time to pay suppliers). They pay a €0.38 dividend (plus an extraordinary dividend in 2025) and have a 10% share buyback authorization. The company is founder-controlled (which creates a key-man risk) and has no mergers and acquisitions (M&A) habit. It is an organic compounder with a lazy balance sheet that provides a soft floor for a potential leveraged buyout (LBO) (they could support 4.0 to 4.5x debt capacity, equal to roughly €900m to €1bn).

Valuation

Driver-based 10-year Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model: Business-to-Business (B2B) growth fading from 9% down to 2.5% over the decade. Digital Profit & Loss (P&L) isolated: Hardware operating profit margin (EBITDA) expands on its own operating leverage to ~20.75%. Digital revenue carries its own ~60% EBITDA margin (stress-tested between 50% and 75%). The total base margin is held at the defensible ~23.5% by 2032. This restructure is margin-neutral, so the rating does not move. The Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC, the discount rate) is 7.75%. This is built from a German Bund risk-free rate of 3.13% + a 0.95 Beta × 4.23% Equity Risk Premium (ERP) + a 0.6% weighted Country Risk Premium (CRP). This avoids double-counting risk by not mixing Italian BTP yields with the CRP. Terminal growth is 2.25%, cross-checked by an exit multiple. The Gordon Growth math implies a ~9.4x exit multiple, which is below today's ~13x trading multiple and below the comparable company range (Johnson Health Tech at 10.5x, Amer Sports at 20.1x, and Garmin at 19.8x bracket the platform debate). Terminal Value (TV) makes up 63% of the total Enterprise Value (EV).

Isolating digital at its true margin shows the platform option is worth ~€3.3 per share (when moving from base case to bull case for digital only), versus ~€0.6 when it was blended into the group margin. This is material, but it sits in the bull case, not the base. The May to July 2026 stock price drop (from 19x down to 13x EBITDA, a 30% decline) was multiple compression due to Americas growth deceleration (Q1 was only +2.5%) and analyst downgrades, not an earnings miss. Our base case sits ~€0.5 above the current stock price, meaning the market de-rate was directionally correct and roughly fair. The Wall Street consensus target of €19.3 to €20.6 implicitly pays for a faster digital ramp and ~24.5% margins.

Risks & Catalysts

Risks (ranked by impact): 1. The B2B capital expenditure cycle turns (a 5 percentage point growth deceleration equals roughly a €3.5 to €4.0 per share value loss). 2. Profit margin stalls (bear case €8.82). 3. Digital thesis fails or they continue to hide the numbers. 4. Americas tariffs (17% of revenue). 5. Key-man risk (founder control). 6. Foreign Exchange (FX) risk.

Catalysts: H1-2026 results (30 July); first digital KPI disclosure (which would define the thesis); Olympic-year pipeline conversion; buyback activation.

What Has To Be True / What Changes My Mind

At the current €15.19 price, the market is pricing in roughly 6 to 8% fading growth, 22 to 23% profit margins, and near-zero digital value in the base case.

Mind-changers to BUY: Americas growth re-accelerates (proving the slowdown was just tariff deferral, not structural) and a first digital disclosure that supports the software option.

Mind-changers to BEAR: Americas confirming they are losing market share; first profit margin decline in five years; a third year of digital silence.

Red-team revisions applied: Widened the point estimate to a range. Moved the rating to HOLD after the IFRS-16 lease principal fix (which lowered value by €1.6/share) and the digital isolation. Promoted the exit-multiple cross-check as the primary terminal value defense. Isolated digital revenue so the bull-to-base spread reflects the software option honestly, rather than artificially inflating the hardware margin endpoint.
