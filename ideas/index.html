How close are we to AGI, actually?
WRITING · Aug 2026

Most public "AGI timeline" takes split into two unhelpful camps: pure benchmark extrapolation that ignores compute, energy, and data constraints, and pure skepticism that ignores how fast capability has actually moved over the last three years. I want to build a real framework instead of picking a side: what specific capabilities would have to be demonstrated, in what order, before "AGI" is a testable claim rather than a marketing term, and where the frontier labs' own roadmaps and public benchmark results actually put us against that framework today.

What does AI actually do to the Indian labour market?
WRITING · Aug 2026

Most commentary on AI and jobs is either US-centric, built around a services and white-collar economy, or generically alarmist about the whole world at once. India's labour market is structurally different: a much larger share of employment sits in informal, low-skill, and IT-services (BPO/ITES) work, some of which is more directly exposed to automation than the US narrative accounts for, while a young population and a low-capital-intensity informal sector may blunt displacement in ways the US case does not capture. I want to work out which specific segments of Indian employment are genuinely exposed, and on what timeline, rather than repeat the general "AI changes everything" framing.

Does the OIS-versus-bond decomposition hold up outside the UK?
OPEN · Aug 2026

The UK Rates edition of Market Signals reads Bank of England policy expectations off the SONIA swap curve rather than gilt yields, because the gap between the two turned out to be worth 72 basis points at the 20-year point, mostly noise that has nothing to do with policy. The obvious next question: does the same gap exist, and tell the same story, on the US Treasury curve against SOFR swaps, or the Euro curve against ESTR? If long-dated Treasury yields are also mostly a front-end story right now rather than a widening fiscal premium, that is a genuinely different read on the "US fiscal crisis" narrative than most commentary gives. I have not pulled the data yet.

Where else does the "isolate the hidden digital business" trick apply?
OPEN · Aug 2026

The Technogym DCF found that blending the software/digital revenue into the group's overall margin was hiding its real value; isolated at its own ~60% margin, the digital option is worth roughly five times what it looked like blended in. That is not really a Technogym-specific insight, it is a general warning about any hardware company that reports a connected-software layer inside one blended segment. Peloton, Garmin, and iFit are the obvious next tests. I have not run any of their numbers, so I don't yet know whether Technogym's clean digital margin was typical or a lucky case.

Grew out of: Technogym DCF
Does bolt-on M&A actually add to LBO returns, or just to the headline story?
OPEN · Aug 2026

The Recordati LBO decomposition found that the celebrated "bolt-on acquisition machine" pharma sponsors like to market adds close to nothing to the annual return rate once the financing cost of the acquisitions is netted against the multiple arbitrage. It grows the total dollars returned, not the percentage return. If that mechanism is not specific to this one deal's numbers, it should show up in other sponsor-backed pharma roll-ups too, and the "acquisitive growth" pitch behind a lot of these deals would be doing less work than it sounds like. I have not tested a second deal yet.

Grew out of: Recordati LBO
