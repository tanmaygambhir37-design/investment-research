UK Rates: Reading the Gilt Curve
VOL. 1 · NO. 1 · AUG 2026
The bond market is betting on about two and a half UK rate rises over the next year. The Bank of England's own policymakers voted 6-3 to hold rates steady this month, and said plainly that financial conditions are already doing the work for them. Reading the right market, not the obvious one, suggests the priced-in rate rises are too aggressive.
The trade: bet on UK rates settling lower than the market currently expects. Profits in almost any scenario short of three rate rises.
