## Methodology

- **Public sources only.** Press coverage, official announcements, review platforms (G2), customer case studies, founder interviews. Every claim traces to a numbered citation.
- **Confidence tiers.** Each data point is graded High / Medium / Low / Not Disclosed. Company-reported figures are treated as directionally credible, never as audited fact.
- **No invented numbers.** Undisclosed metrics (gross margin, runway, CAC/LTV) are shown as "Not Disclosed" and framed as risks, not estimated into existence.
- **Base rates over stories.** Outcomes are underwritten against comparable-company history, with explicit analyst-assigned scenario probabilities and falsifiable "what would change our mind" triggers.
